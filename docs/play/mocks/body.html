What is real here? ↗

This is a playable visual mock. Bread and coffee are scenario cues. Shapes, speeds, quantities and glow are authored; they do not predict a meal's effect on your body.

Real interaction: adding contents, queueing a sequence, local wave impulses, toy bolus movement, replay and a gut close-up. Neural flashes and blood-borne particles are separate. A ripple is a player-controlled abstraction, not an externally controllable human reflex.

The enteric nervous system can coordinate local movement. Caffeine acts through receptor antagonism; it does not add energy. The coffee path ends at a symbolic receptor gate; binding is not simulated. Absorption and neural signaling do not share the same biological clock.

Sources: NIDDK digestion · Spencer et al. (mouse colon) · Caffeine A1 occupancy.
