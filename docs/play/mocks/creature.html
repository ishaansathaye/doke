What is actually happening?

This is an original interaction mock with a fictional controller, not a simulated animal brain.

Three sensors sample food and walls. Their outputs control turning and speed. Contact increases a bounded avoidance gain; it slowly relaxes afterward. That is the only adaptation implemented.

The body follows its traveled path. Internal light shows continuous sensor and motor activity; moving points are visual encodings, not action potentials. No synaptic learning, chemistry, biomechanics, or consciousness is modeled.

Keyboard: 1 / 2 / 3 select tools, Space pauses, F drops food, W adds a barrier, C clears, R resets. Touch or drag anywhere in the world to paint.
